# Epic 2 – Route Planning

## E2-S01 — Planning Analysis Engine

### Goal

Create the domain service that analyses a completed route and produces planning metrics before execution.

This service is the heart of the planning phase and provides deterministic analysis without mutating game state.

### Deliverable

Planning analysis service and result model.

### Scope

- Route traversal.
- Travel time estimation.
- Delay probability calculation.
- Damage probability calculation.
- Maximum reward estimation inputs.

### Out of Scope

- SwiftUI presentation.
- Route execution.
- Settlement.

### Dependencies

- E1-S06.
- E5-S02.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Accepts an immutable route and job configuration.
- Produces a presentation-independent analysis result.
- Never consumes random numbers.
- Returns identical output for identical inputs.
- Keeps analysis policies outside SwiftUI.
- Includes route-change and determinism unit tests.

## E2-S02 — Planning Summary Presentation

### Goal

Present the analysis generated by the Planning Analysis Engine to the player.

### Deliverable

Planning Summary UI.

### Scope

- Estimated arrival.
- Target Time.
- Deadline.
- Delay exposure.
- Damage risk.
- Maximum reward.

### Out of Scope

- Planning calculations.
- Route recommendation.
- Execution.

### Dependencies

- E2-S03.
- E2-S04.
- E2-S05.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Displays every canonical planning metric.
- Distinguishes Target Time and Deadline.
- Updates immediately when the route changes.
- Layout remains stable across supported iPhone sizes.
- Does not rank or recommend routes.
- Includes UI tests for live metric updates.

## E2-S03 — Delay Exposure Classification

### Goal

Implement the canonical delay exposure classification used by the planning summary.

### Deliverable

Delay Exposure Classifier.

### Scope

- Probability calculation.
- Low / Medium / High classification.
- Threshold configuration.

### Out of Scope

- Damage risk.
- UI.
- Random delay resolution.

### Dependencies

- E2-S01.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Uses the canonical independent-probability formula.
- Classifies below 25% as Low.
- Classifies 25% to below 50% as Medium.
- Classifies 50% or above as High.
- Produces deterministic classifications.
- Includes boundary and multi-card unit tests.

## E2-S04 — Damage Risk Classification

### Goal

Implement the canonical damage risk classification.

### Deliverable

Damage Risk Classifier.

### Scope

- Damage probability calculation.
- Low / Medium / High classification.
- Heavy Traffic probability handling.

### Out of Scope

- Delay exposure.
- UI.
- Damage resolution.

### Dependencies

- E2-S01.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Uses 7.5% overall damage probability per Heavy Traffic card.
- Uses the canonical probability formula.
- Classifies below 10% as Low.
- Classifies 10% to below 25% as Medium.
- Classifies 25% or above as High.
- Includes boundary and multi-card unit tests.

## E2-S05 — Maximum Reward Estimation

### Goal

Estimate the highest achievable reward for the currently planned route.

### Deliverable

Maximum Reward Estimator.

### Scope

- Economy integration.
- Early bonus.
- Lateness prediction.
- Damage expectation excluded.

### Out of Scope

- Expected payout.
- Final settlement.

### Dependencies

- E2-S01.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Uses shared economy configuration.
- Applies canonical timing boundaries.
- Excludes unresolved damage and random delay penalties.
- Returns zero when deterministic arrival is at or after Deadline.
- Never exceeds the theoretical maximum for equivalent inputs.
- Includes early, on-target, late and failure unit tests.

## E2-S06 — Route Confirmation

### Goal

Lock the analysed route and transition the game into execution.

### Deliverable

Planning → Execution Transition and confirmed-route model.

### Scope

- Route confirmation.
- Immutable route creation.
- Planning state finalisation.
- Execution hand-off.

### Out of Scope

- Route execution.
- Outcome calculation.

### Dependencies

- E2-S02.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Confirms only complete routes.
- Creates an immutable execution input.
- Prevents editing after confirmation.
- Starts execution with the confirmed route only.
- Prevents duplicate transitions.
- Includes transition and UI tests.
