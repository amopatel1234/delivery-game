# Delivery Game Roadmap

## Vision
Build a polished route-planning game where interesting decisions come from balancing reward, travel time and risk.

---

## MVP (Current)

### Milestone 1 – Foundation
- Project setup
- Grid model
- Card model
- Deterministic seeded jobs

### Milestone 2 – Gameplay
- Route planning
- Route validation
- Simulation engine
- Timing system
- Reward calculation
- Results screen

### Milestone 3 – Content
- Five authored seeded jobs
- Balancing pass
- Tutorial/onboarding

### Milestone 4 – TestFlight
- Internal testing
- External TestFlight
- Player feedback
- Balance iteration

Success criteria:
- Players understand the mechanics.
- Route choices feel meaningful.
- Players replay jobs to improve outcomes.

---

## Post-MVP
- Random job generation
- Additional card types
- Progression and economy expansion
- Vehicles and upgrades
- Daily challenges
- Statistics
- Accessibility improvements
- Visual polish and audio

---

## Long-term
- Business management
- Multiple cities
- Campaign mode
- Leaderboards
- iPad optimisation
